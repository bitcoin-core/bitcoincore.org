--- name: preciousblock btcversion: 28.2.0 btcgroup: blockchain permalink: en/doc/28.2.0/rpc/blockchain/preciousblock/raw/ rawpage: true --- preciousblock "blockhash" Treats a block as if it were received before others with the same work. A later preciousblock call can override the effect of an earlier one. The effects of preciousblock are not retained across restarts. Arguments: 1. blockhash (string) the hash of the block to mark as precious Result: (null)
