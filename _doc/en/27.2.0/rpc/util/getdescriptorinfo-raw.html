--- name: getdescriptorinfo btcversion: 27.2.0 btcgroup: util permalink: en/doc/27.2.0/rpc/util/getdescriptorinfo/raw/ rawpage: true --- getdescriptorinfo "descriptor" Analyses a descriptor. Arguments: 1. descriptor (string) The descriptor. Result: (object) descriptor (string) The descriptor in canonical form, without private keys checksum (string) The checksum for the input descriptor isrange (boolean) Whether the descriptor is ranged issolvable (boolean) Whether the descriptor is solvable hasprivatekeys (boolean) Whether the input descriptor contained at least one private key
