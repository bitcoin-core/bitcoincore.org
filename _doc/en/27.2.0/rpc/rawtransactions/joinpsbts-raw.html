--- name: joinpsbts btcversion: 27.2.0 btcgroup: rawtransactions permalink: en/doc/27.2.0/rpc/rawtransactions/joinpsbts/raw/ rawpage: true --- joinpsbts txs Joins multiple distinct PSBTs with different inputs and outputs into one PSBT with inputs and outputs from all of the PSBTs No input in any of the PSBTs can be in more than one of the PSBTs. Arguments: 1. txs (array) The base64 strings of partially signed transactions each item (string) Result: (string)
