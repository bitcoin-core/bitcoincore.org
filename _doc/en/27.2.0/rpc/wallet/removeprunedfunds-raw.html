--- name: removeprunedfunds btcversion: 27.2.0 btcgroup: wallet permalink: en/doc/27.2.0/rpc/wallet/removeprunedfunds/raw/ rawpage: true --- removeprunedfunds "txid" Deletes the specified transaction from the wallet. Meant for use with pruned wallets and as a companion to importprunedfunds. This will affect wallet balances. Arguments: 1. txid (string) The hex-encoded id of the transaction you are deleting Result: (null)
