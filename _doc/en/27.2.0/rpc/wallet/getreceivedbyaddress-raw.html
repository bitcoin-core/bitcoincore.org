--- name: getreceivedbyaddress btcversion: 27.2.0 btcgroup: wallet permalink: en/doc/27.2.0/rpc/wallet/getreceivedbyaddress/raw/ rawpage: true --- getreceivedbyaddress "address" ( minconf ) ( include_immature_coinbase ) Returns the total amount received by the given address in transactions with at least minconf confirmations. Arguments: 1. address (string) The bitcoin address for transactions. 2. minconf (number, optional, default=1) Only include transactions confirmed at least this many times. 3. include_immature_coinbase (boolean, optional, default=false) Include immature coinbase transactions. Result: (string, amount)
