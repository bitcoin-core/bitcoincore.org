rawtransactions

combinerawtransaction txs

Combine multiple partially signed transactions into one transaction. The combined transaction may be another partially signed transaction or a fully signed transaction.

Arguments

#NameTypeRequiredDescription
1txsarrayyesThe hex strings of partially signed transactions
  • each item (string)

Result

(string)

Examples

> bitcoin-cli combinerawtransaction '["myhex1", "myhex2", "myhex3"]'
